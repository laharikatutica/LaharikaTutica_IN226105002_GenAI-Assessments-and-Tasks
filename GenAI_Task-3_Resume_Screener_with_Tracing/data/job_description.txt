Role: Data Scientist

We are looking for a Data Scientist who can analyze data, build machine learning models, and generate actionable business insights.

---

Required Skills:
- Python programming
- SQL for data querying and manipulation
- Data Analysis and statistical understanding
- Machine Learning basics (supervised and unsupervised learning)
- Data Visualization (matplotlib, seaborn, or similar tools)

---

Preferred Skills:
- Pandas and NumPy
- NLP (Natural Language Processing)
- Experience with ML libraries such as Scikit-learn
- Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
- Experience with model deployment
- 3+ years of experience in Data Analytics or related field

---

Responsibilities:
- Clean, analyze, and interpret large datasets
- Build and evaluate machine learning models
- Create dashboards and data visualizations
- Work with structured and unstructured data
- Communicate insights to stakeholders
- Collaborate with engineering and business teams

---

Education:
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, or related field preferred

---

Note:
Strong candidates will have a combination of technical skills, practical experience, and the ability to apply machine learning techniques to real-world problems.
